You have real experience and a generic resume

Most tools rewrite you to fit the posting. That works right up until an interviewer asks a follow-up about something you never did — and then the whole application is suspect, not just that line.

This works the other way round. You write down what actually happened, once. Every output — the match report, the bullets, the eight stories — gets assembled from that record and nothing else. When the material is thin, it says so instead of filling the gap for you.
